# Jordi Pastry: Owner's Guide

Your dashboard lives at **your-website/admin**. Add it to your phone's home screen: in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome, tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen**.

On a phone, the menu is at the bottom: **Overview, Orders, Products, Analytics** and **More** (everything else).

---

## When a new order comes in

1. You get a Telegram message: **🍩 NEW JORDI ORDER**.
2. Tap **💬 WhatsApp Customer** if you want to say hello. The message is already written; just press send.
3. Tap **📋 View Order** (or open **Orders**) to see everything.

**Overview → Needs your attention** always shows orders waiting for you.

## Confirm an InstaPay payment

The customer pays by InstaPay and taps **I've Sent the Payment**. You get a **💸 PAYMENT REPORTED** message.

1. **Open your InstaPay app first** and check that the money really arrived: the right amount, from this customer.
2. Open the order and tap the green **Confirm Payment** button.
3. Tick **"I checked my InstaPay app and received EGP …"** and tap **Yes, payment received**.

Didn't receive it? Tap **Reject Payment Report**, write a short reason (e.g. "We can't see your transfer yet"), and confirm. The customer sees your reason and can send it again.

> Never confirm based on a screenshot alone. Screenshots can be faked. Only your InstaPay app is proof.

## Prepare and hand over an order

On the order page, the big button always shows the next step:

**Start Preparing → Mark Ready → Out for Delivery** (delivery orders only) **→ Complete Order**

The customer's tracking page updates by itself at each step. Tip: confirm payment before you start preparing.

## Contact a customer

On any order: **WhatsApp** opens a chat with a message matched to the order's status, and **Call** opens your phone dialer.

## Cancel an order

Order page → **Cancel order** (at the bottom of "Next step") → confirm. Any limited stock goes back on the menu. If they already paid, send the money back, then tap **Mark refunded**.

## Add a donut

1. **Products → Add Product.**
2. Fill in the **name**, a short **description**, the **price** and the **category**.
3. Tap **Add photo** and pick a picture (square photos look best; they're resized automatically).
4. Tap **Save Product**. It's on the website straight away.

Optional extras on the same page:

- **Sale price:** shows the old price crossed out.
- **Choices:** e.g. *Regular / Mini (x3)*. The customer must pick one.
- **Toppings:** optional extras with their own price.
- **Ingredients & allergens:** only add what you know for sure.
- **Limit how many I can sell:** it shows "Sold out" automatically when they run out.
- **Custom boxes:** which boxes this flavor can go in, plus an optional premium surcharge.

## Change a price

**Products** → tap the donut → change **Price** → **Save Changes**. The website updates immediately. Old orders keep the price they were placed at.

## Mark a donut as sold out

**Products** → on the donut's row, tap the green **Available** pill. It changes to **Sold out**. Tap it again when you have more.

- **Visible / Hidden** hides a donut from the website without deleting it.
- **★ Featured** puts it in the homepage showcase.
- **⋮ → Archive** takes it off the menu but keeps it for later. **⋮ → Duplicate** copies it as a starting point.

## Sample products

The store starts with a few **Sample** donuts so it doesn't look empty. Edit them into your real menu (saving removes the "Sample" label) or delete them before you share the website.

## See your sales

- **Overview:** today's orders, what needs attention, and today's and this month's sales.
- **Analytics:** orders per day, revenue per week and month, your most popular flavors (including those in boxes), and payment status. Switch between 7, 30 and 90 days.

Sales only count **payments you confirmed**. Unconfirmed InstaPay transfers are never counted.

**Orders → More filters & export → Export CSV** downloads your orders for Excel or Google Sheets.

## Close orders temporarily

**Overview → Accepting orders:** switch it off. Customers see your closing message at the top of the website and can't check out. Switch it on to reopen.

Change the message in **Store Settings → General → Message while closed**.

To limit how many orders you take per day: **Store Settings → Inventory & capacity → Limit orders per day**.

InstaPay orders that are never paid are canceled automatically after 24 hours, so they stop holding your stock. Change the hours (or set 0 to turn this off) in **Store Settings → Inventory & capacity**. If a customer tapped "I've Sent the Payment", the order is never canceled automatically; you decide.

## Change your website

**Website Editor:**

- **Homepage:** headline, text, buttons, hero photo, which sections show and in what order, featured donuts.
- **Branding:** logo, small logo mark, browser icon, store name, tagline, and brand colors (all pre-checked to stay readable).
- **About:** your story and photo.
- **Footer & contact:** WhatsApp, phone, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, area and hours.
- **Announcement:** a slim bar at the top, e.g. "Fresh Donuts Available Today!"
- **Reviews:** anyone can leave a review from the homepage (**Give us a review**) and it appears right away, labelled *Customer review*. Customers can also review from their order page after you tap **Complete Order**; those show a *Verified order* badge. To remove one, tap **Hide** or **Delete**.

## Store settings

- **Payment:** InstaPay on/off, your InstaPay name and address, and cash on/off.
- **Delivery:** delivery and pickup on/off, delivery areas with fees, minimum order, pickup instructions (only shown to the customer who ordered), and whether customers can choose a day and time.
- **Inventory:** daily order limit, and when to remind you about unpaid orders.

## Telegram alerts not arriving?

**Notifications** shows whether Telegram is connected, has a **Send Test Notification** button, and lists any messages that failed, with a **Retry** button. Orders are always saved even when Telegram has a problem.

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot your password?** and follow the email link.
